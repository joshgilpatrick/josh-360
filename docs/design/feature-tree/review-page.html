What comes into josh-360

Every feature in action-triage, claude-360 and macbook-automations, plus the agent-deck hooks on your laptop, as a tree. A ticked box means the feature is extracted and built into josh-360 as part of one app with one look; an unticked box means it is not brought in. Each call has its reason beside it.

Your review of 17 November is written in: every box now shows the call you made, and my reply sits under each of your comments. You can still tick, untick and comment; changes save as you go, and Send review tells me to write them in again.
