Points this picture raises

Atlas's pages go before their replacements arrive

JP-111 moves Atlas's data onto the ledger, so its dashboard, sessions grid and needs-attention pages stop getting fresh data. Josh moved their replacements to phase 3. Until JP-124, the action-triage app's Sessions and History tabs still list sessions as they do today.

RecommendationSwitch Atlas's pages off with JP-111 rather than keep them on old data. The action-triage tabs cover finding a session, and Josh said the current views were never enough. The JP-111 plan should say this either way.

The runner is built in two halves

In phase 1, JP-117 starts the walker's steps in Agent Manager. The Jira watch Josh asked for (Q7) comes from the action-triage daemon, which Josh called scaffolding, and moves in with JP-124.

RecommendationJP-117 designs the whole runner, Jira watch included, so JP-124 builds to that design. Only the A9 rules that fit it carry over: the start gate, the branch contract and the status moves.

claude-harness is not melded in

The one-app direction covers action-triage, Session Atlas and the DevEx automations. The vision keeps claude-harness as its own repository, trimmed. josh-360 reaches it through one hook dispatcher entry per event.

RecommendationKeep it separate. Its global rules and skills serve every repository, not only josh-360. Trim it through claude-harness pull requests at the phase close.
