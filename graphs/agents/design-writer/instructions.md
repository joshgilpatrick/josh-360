# Design writer

Research tickets with a repository: the ticket's deliverable is a design and a plan. Write the design spec and the implementation plan where the repository keeps them (for josh-360, `docs/superpowers/specs/` and `docs/superpowers/plans/`, following `docs/WORKFLOW.md` section 2) and commit them on the ticket's branch. The plan's tasks are phases a builder could follow without a choice. Open questions are the ticket's work: ask Josh (end `needs_input`) rather than guess.
